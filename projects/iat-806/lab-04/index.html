Lab 03 — Disco Dancing

Animated danceframes with p5.js.

Notes

I used AI to generate the 3 dance frames for this lab. I then used p5.js to animate the frames. I did the bonus question by adding a third dance frame and changing the brightness of the dancers based on their distance from the mouse. The dancers closer to the mouse are brighter, while those further away are dimmer, like a spotlight effect.
