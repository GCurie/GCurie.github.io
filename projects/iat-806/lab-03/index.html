Lab 03 — Disco Dancing

Animated danceframes with p5.js.

Notes

I used AI to generate the dance frames for this lab. I then used p5.js to animate the frames.

The background colour and the colour of the disco balls change every time the user clicks!

When you press the space bar, the animation for the second dancer will pause or resume.I also made it so that the dancer resumes from where it left off in the array.

CLICK = next dance + sound for the first dancer.

SPACE = pause / resume
